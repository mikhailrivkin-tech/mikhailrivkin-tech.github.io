Privacy Policy

This policy describes the private, owner-operated BB Google Calendar integration. It is not offered as a public service.

1. What Google data is accessed

With the owner's Google OAuth permission, the integration can access the calendars and events permitted by the granted Google Calendar scopes, including event titles, descriptions, dates, times, calendars, and related identifiers. It may create, update or delete Google Calendar events when the owner uses the corresponding functions.

2. Why data is used

Data is used to show the owner's Google Calendar events inside BB, synchronize events between Google Calendar and local BB records, and perform the calendar-management actions the owner requests. The integration does not use Google Calendar data for targeted advertising or sale of personal information.

3. Storage and access

Calendar records may be cached in the owner's private BB local database. Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the BB application's local configuration so that synchronization can continue without requiring a browser login for each request. Access to the underlying system is controlled by the owner; this public website does not collect or store calendar contents or OAuth tokens.

4. Sharing and processing

Calendar data is exchanged with Google Calendar as needed for synchronization and owner-requested changes. Locally stored calendar information can be displayed and used through the owner's BB workspace, including BB tools when the owner chooses to invoke them. The integration operator does not sell Google data or share it with advertisers. No additional external calendar-data processing recipient is configured or represented by this website.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally synchronized calendar records and authorization configuration are kept for the owner's continued use until removed by the owner or the application's cleanup procedures. Google Calendar remains the system of record for events stored in the Google account. Disconnecting Google access does not, by itself, guarantee deletion of copies already cached locally; the owner manages removal of local data.

6. Revoke authorization

The owner can revoke this application's access at Google Account — third-party connections. Revoking authorization prevents future Google Calendar API access until the owner authorizes the application again.

7. Public website visitors

These public pages provide application and privacy information only. They have no sign-in, input forms or tracking scripts set by the site operator. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may process normal site access logs under its own terms.

8. Policy updates and contact

This policy will be updated if the application's data use, storage or sharing changes materially. Contact the operator using the public GitHub issue tracker without including personal events, credentials or other sensitive information.
