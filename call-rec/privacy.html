Privacy Policy

Call Rec Pipeline is a personal, self-hosted application used by its owner to process their own call-recording files. It is not a service offered to the public.

1. Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive files and metadata. Its current OAuth permission is broad enough to read, create, update, and delete Drive files, although its presently tested workflow only lists and downloads files from the owner-selected test folder. Actual operations depend on which functions the owner explicitly enables.

2. Purpose and use

The application accesses recordings to detect incoming calls, download recordings, verify file integrity, and, when the transcription workflow is enabled, create text transcripts for the owner's private use. Google Drive data is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

3. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in restricted local configuration on the owner's server, not on this public website. Audio files, related metadata, and generated transcripts may be held in private server storage to support processing, recovery, and verification. Access is restricted to the owner's designated system accounts. The website itself does not upload or store recordings.

4. Sharing and processing

Drive content is not sold, rented, or made available to advertisers. Processing is performed on owner-controlled systems using configured tools. If the owner later enables an external processing or document-ingestion service, data will be sent only to that owner-selected service for that specific function; the privacy disclosures will be updated to reflect any enabled external sharing.

5. Retention and deletion

Google Drive originals remain in the owner's account unless the owner explicitly authorizes a separate retention action. Local working copies may be retained until processing and verified delivery are complete, subject to the owner's configured cleanup rules. The app does not promise a fixed retention period at this development stage. The owner can remove local data and revoke app access at any time.

6. Revoking Google access

Access may be revoked through the owner's Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future API calls that require authorization but does not automatically erase copies previously stored locally; those are managed by the owner.

7. Website visitors

These public information pages contain no application login, cookies configured by the developer, tracking scripts, or web forms. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary access logs under its own terms.

8. Changes and contact

This policy will be revised if actual processing, data recipients, or retention practices materially change. Contact the operator through the public repository's issue tracker without disclosing private information.
