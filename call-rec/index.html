Call Rec Pipeline

A personal utility that connects to the account owner's Google Drive to retrieve and process their own phone-call recordings on a private server.

What the application does

It is being developed to identify new audio recordings in the owner's designated Google Drive folder, download files for integrity checks, and produce transcription documents using locally configured processing tools. A private document handoff may be enabled by the owner in a later stage.

How Google Drive is used

The app uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API. The current OAuth permission allows broad Drive access, even though the operating workflow is configured to use specific owner-selected folders. This access is used only for the owner's recording-management workflow.

Current operating status

Development and read-only Drive tests are underway. This site provides application information and does not itself request Google authentication or receive recordings.

Your privacy: This is a personal, self-operated tool, not a public service. Read the Privacy Policy for how Google user data is accessed, handled, and protected.
